Evaluation Methodology & Tolerances

1. Final Similarity Score

The system computes a final similarity score based on dimensional and quantitative limits specified by the Indian Standard (IS) codes. The final score maps to the following quality matrix:

Similarity Score (%) Quality Status Action Required
> 95ExcellentAcceptable, no action.
90 – 95AcceptableAcceptable, no action.
80 – 89.9Minor DeviationReview Required. No email sent.
70 – 79.9Major DeviationEmail Alert Triggered.
< 70DefectiveEmail Alert Triggered.
Top View Score Formula
Final Score = (Rc + Rr + Rs) / 3
Side View Score Formula
Final Score = Average(Rs_all_pairs)

Note: Any final score below 80% automatically flags the overall column as requiring intervention, triggering an automated email alert.

  • 16 × dia of smallest longitudinal bar
  • 300 mm

3. Processing Methodology

Top View Evaluation:

Extracts the main perimeter rods, verifies the exact count (Rc), calculates the median localized pixel radius (Rr) mapped to mm, and assesses the sequential perimeter gaps (Rs) between consecutive rods in a clockwise arrangement.

Side View Evaluation:

Isolates the foreground horizontal ties (stirrups). The evaluation focuses on the vertical pitch spacing (Rs) between consecutive bars. Checks are made against the IS 456 limit (least of column dim, 16× dia, or 300mm) and designed spacing (±10mm/±15mm). If any interval exceeds limits, it drops to 0%, affecting the whole average.
